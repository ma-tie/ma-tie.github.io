Hi, my name is Tie Ma (马铁).

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at [Beihang University](https://ev.buaa.edu.cn/) (BUAA), advised by Prof. [Shan Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Low-un4AAAAJ) and [Hongbin Luo](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=aaEjzHMAAAAJ). I was a visiting student at [KAUST](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/) from September 2025 to September 2026, advised by Prof. [Marco Canini](https://mcanini.github.io/). My research interests focus on **intelligent resource management and scheduling for AI infrastructure, as well as scalable experimentation platforms for AI systems**.

Prior to Beihang University, I received my M.S. from [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/) (UESTC) in 2023, under the guidance of Prof. [Hongfang Yu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GmEdMqwAAAAJ) and [Long Luo](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=rcqa1EEAAAAJ). I received my B.S. from [Xidian University](https://en.xidian.edu.cn/) in 2020.
